Important guest information

Drinking water: The filtered well water is highly mineralized. Bring your own drinking water.

Greywater: The bathroom sink and shower use a greywater system. Use only provided soaps and shampoos, and take short showers.

Quiet: Sound carries through the canyons. Respect neighbors; normal-volume conversation and quiet music are fine.

Smoking: Outdoors only. Close windows when smoking on the deck and remove all cigarette butts.

Pets: Not permitted.
