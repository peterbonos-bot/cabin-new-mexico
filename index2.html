The cabin

A quiet place to land in the high desert

This cabin sits at the edge of the piñon hills, built for anyone who wants mornings that start with coffee on the porch and evenings that end under more stars than a city sky ever shows. It's close enough to town for supplies and a good meal, and far enough that the quiet actually reaches you.

Guests tend to remember the same things: the crackle of the fireplace, the smell of piñon smoke, and how little there is to do here except exactly what you came to do. Whether that's hiking, reading on the porch, or nothing at all, the cabin gets out of the way and lets you have it.
