The cabin

A mountain cabin with a New Mexico soul

Set in the Jemez Mountains near Jemez Springs, the cabin offers mountain quiet and wide New Mexico skies. Enjoy coffee on the deck, the surrounding landscape, and peaceful, dark nights.

Sky Cabin has metal siding and is relatively near neighboring homes while retaining a comfortable sense of privacy. Nights are very quiet and dark. The cabin is heated by baseboard heaters. Piñon and juniper firewood is provided for guests. There is no Wi-Fi at Sky Cabin, but cellular data service is available in the area. Pets are not allowed at Sky Cabin.

Getting here

Sky Cabin is in the Jemez Mountains, approximately 10 minutes by car from Jemez Springs. The final approach is a rough gravel road, and an SUV is recommended. The cabin is private and quiet, with some neighboring homes nearby. Allow approximately 1 hour 15 minutes to drive from Santa Fe or Albuquerque International Sunport, depending on conditions.

Before you arrive

A few things to know

Adults only: Sky Cabin accommodates one or two adult guests sharing one queen bed. Pets are not permitted.

Water: Water is precious here. Tap water comes from a filtered well but is highly mineralized; please bring your own drinking water.

Greywater: The bathroom sink and shower drain to a greywater system. Use only the soaps and shampoos provided, and please take short showers.

Smoking: Smoking is permitted outdoors only. Close the windows when smoking on the deck, and remove all cigarette butts.

Quiet surroundings: Sound travels through the canyons. Please respect neighbors by keeping noise low. Normal-volume conversation and quiet music are fine.

Fees: A separate cleaning fee applies; its amount will be provided before a booking is confirmed.
