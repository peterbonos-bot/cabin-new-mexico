Stay a While

The cabin has one queen bedroom, a galley kitchen, and wraparound decks. Neighboring homes are nearby, but the cabin feels private. Nights are quiet and dark.

There’s room to cook a simple meal, read, or have coffee on the deck. Jemez Springs is about ten minutes away for hot springs and places to eat.

Explore the Region

Getting Here

Sky Cabin is approximately 10 minutes from Jemez Springs, with trails and natural or developed hot springs in the surrounding area.

  • Jemez Springs: cafés, a bakery, a brewery, and a pizzeria; fine-dining options are limited.
  • Santa Fe or Albuquerque: approximately 1 hour 20 minutes by car, depending on conditions.
  • Taos: approximately 2 hours 40 minutes by car.
  • The final approach: a rough gravel road and unpaved parking. An SUV is recommended.

Before You Arrive

A Few Things to Know.

Guests. Adults only, maximum two guests sharing one queen bed. Pets are not permitted.

Drinking water. The filtered well water is highly mineralized. Please bring your own drinking water.

Greywater. The bathroom sink and shower use a greywater system. Use only provided soaps and shampoos, and take short showers.

Smoking. Outdoors only. Close windows when smoking on the deck and remove all cigarette butts.

Quiet. Sound carries through the canyons. Please respect neighbors and keep noise low.

Booking details. Check-in is at 3 PM; check-out is at 11 AM. A separate cleaning fee applies; the amount is provided before confirmation.
